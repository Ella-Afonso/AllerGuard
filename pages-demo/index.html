How AllerGuard works
  1. Reviews public FSA recall alerts against recorded inventory.
  2. Keeps unrelated alerts quiet, but records them.
  3. Escalates relevant or uncertain alerts for an owner decision.
  4. Records evidence and diary entries.
Safety boundaries
  • Decisions record a choice.
  • No stock or customer action is executed.
  • Specialist proposals and notifications are simulated in this public browser demo.
